Exam-day tips

Before the exam

  • Review key ideas and formulas using active recall.
  • Pack permitted stationery and check the timetable.
  • Eat normally, hydrate, and get enough sleep.
  • Avoid trying to learn every topic at the last minute.

During the exam

  • Read instructions carefully before answering.
  • Plan time according to marks and question difficulty.
  • Start with questions you can answer confidently.
  • Leave time to check calculations and omitted questions.

For tests and quizzes

  • Practise a few questions without looking at notes.
  • Review mistakes and understand why an answer was wrong.
  • Revisit difficult topics after a short interval.

After the exam

  • Note topics that need further practice.
  • Use mistakes as information for your next study plan.
  • Take a short reset before returning to your routine.
